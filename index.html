Model compressionEscha Labs · Summer 2026

Mixed Precision Quantization for 2 Bit LLMs

At Escha Labs I built a method that decides which weights in a language model get extra bits, and the team built it into the quantization pipeline behind its public 2 bit models.

The method scores each layer by how much quantizing it changes the model's output distribution (KL divergence) instead of by weight curvature. At 2.42 bits per weight it improved the team's 2 bit model by 2.4% on commonsense and 2.1% on math and code benchmarks, and beat Hessian guided allocation on 5 of 6 held out Qwen3 8B tasks at the same bit budget. My solver benchmarks led the team to add a code search method 14× faster than its previous baseline. I also reimplemented QTIP from scratch in PyTorch and caught a bug that made the 35B model return empty answers before its public release.

LLM inferenceIndependent research · 2026

Low Bit LLM Inference on NVIDIA A40

I profiled a 27B model quantized to 2 to 3 bits per weight on a single A40 to find out where decode and prefill time actually go.

The best kernel path cut batch 1 decode from 45.3 to 25.7 ms per step, a 1.76× speedup. For speculative decoding I found that the low bit GEMV kernel only stays cheap up to 16 rows, so I capped total draft rows with concurrency, which raised throughput 42% at four concurrent requests. For large prefill batches, decoding the weights once and running plain FP16 matmul beat the fused low bit kernels by 13 to 18%, and I used Nsight Compute instruction counts to explain where the fused path loses time.

Scientific MLResearch project · 2026

Diffusion Models for Cosmological Simulation Fields

I trained conditional diffusion models on CAMELS simulation fields and showed that samples which look right can still be copies of the training set, or ignore the physics parameter they were asked for.

Across 50+ runs from 64 to 32,768 training fields, 95% recovery intervals for Ωm contained the requested value only 22% of the time at 128 fields and 91% at 16,384. Accepted at ML4PS 2026.

CosmologyDESI · 2022–present

Model-Agnostic Tests of Gravity with DESI

Most tests of gravity on cosmic scales fit a one-parameter model, and I showed that model hides a real signal in current data.

A flexible reconstruction gives the first model-agnostic 3σ evidence for modified gravity from DESI, supernova, and CMB data combined (PRD Letter, 79 citations). I am second author on DESI's official 2024 modified gravity analysis.
