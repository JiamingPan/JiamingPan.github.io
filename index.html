LLM systemsEscha Labs · 2026

Low-Bit Quantization and Task-Aware Model Compression

I reimplemented the QTIP quantization pipeline for Qwen3-8B end to end and reduced full-model quantization time from about 8 hours to 2.5 hours. I benchmarked exact-search, iterative local-search, and GPU candidate solvers for the binary least-squares reconstruction step. That analysis informed the solver choice for a downstream team kernel, which achieved a 14× speedup over the previous baseline.

I am also developing task-aware mixed-precision methods that use hidden-state and output-distribution diagnostics to identify the small set of weights that warrant additional bits.

PyTorch · CUDA · QTIP · low-bit evaluation

Post-trainingIndependent study · 2026

RL Post-Training, Quantization, and vLLM Serving

I built a reproducible single-GPU study of whether GRPO post-training gains survive 4-bit AWQ quantization on Qwen2.5-1.5B. The project covers reward and advantage diagnostics, pre- and post-quantization evaluation, and vLLM benchmarks for latency and throughput.

Scientific MLResearch project · 2025–2026

Diffusion Models for Cosmological Simulation Fields

I built the data and evaluation pipeline for diffusion models trained on CAMELS cosmological fields. The evaluation separates memorization from generalization and tests power-spectrum fidelity, learned-feature agreement, and recovery of the physical parameters used to condition the simulations.
